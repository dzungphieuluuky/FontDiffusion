4. Conclusion & Recommendations

  1. Training Efficiency: If computational resources are limited, stop after Phase 1. The difference between 15k Phase 1 and the Full training is minimal (LPIPS 0.07). You achieve ~90% of the visual structure from Phase 1 alone.
  2. Model Selection Strategy:
    • If your dataset is similar to the author's: Use Finetuned (5P1-5P2).
    • If you need precise structural control based only on your data: Train from Scratch. The Finetuned model retains "ghosts" of the original weights.
  3. Fixing Phase 2 Instability: The extreme histogram outliers suggest Phase 2 (15,000 steps) is too aggressive for certain styles.
    Action: Consider reducing the learning rate for Phase 2 or implementing gradient clipping to prevent the contrast explosions observed in the analysis.
