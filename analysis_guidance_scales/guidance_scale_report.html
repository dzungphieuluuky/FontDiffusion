Ablation Study: Guidance Scale Analysis

Model: 15P1-15P2 (Trained from Scratch) | Scales Evaluated: 3 - 12
🚀 Executive Summary: The Sweet Spot is Scale 8

The analysis of 45 pairwise comparisons indicates that Guidance Scale 8 offers the optimal balance between structural integrity and perceptual quality for this model.

  • Optimal Range: Scales 6 through 9 produce highly consistent and high-quality results.
  • Peak Performance: Scale 8 achieves the highest SSIM (0.82) and among the lowest LPIPS scores.
  • Degradation Points:
    • Low (< 5): Results are perceptually generic (Lower SSIM).
    • High (> 10): Severe artifacting occurs. Histogram Distance explodes at Scale 11 and 12, indicating "deep-fried" or over-saturated outputs.

📈 Trend Analysis

1. LPIPS (Perceptual Similarity) - Lower is Better

The LPIPS line plot exhibits a clear "U-shape" curve.

Observation: The error bars (Standard Deviation) are tightest around Scales 6-9. The curve bottoms out (best performance) at Scale 8 (mean ~0.056) and Scale 6 (mean ~0.057). At Scale 12, LPIPS spikes to >0.08, indicating a departure from natural perceptual quality.

2. SSIM (Structural Similarity) - Higher is Better

The SSIM plot shows an inverted "U-shape", validating the LPIPS findings.

Observation: Structural integrity peaks at Scale 8 (Mean ~0.797) and Scale 9. There is a sharp drop-off starting at Scale 11, suggesting that high guidance forces the model into structural deformations.

3. Histogram Distance (Color Distribution) - Lower is Better

This metric reveals the stability of the generated image contrast.

Critical Finding: While stable from scales 3 to 7, the distance increases significantly at Scale 8 and skyrockets at Scales 11 and 12 (reaching >11.0). This confirms that high guidance scales cause contrast artifacts (saturation/frying). Scale 7 is the most "stable" regarding color distribution.
